# Figure 1 — Multi-Layer Dependency Architecture

## System Overview

The Sextant Protocol models infrastructure as a multi-layer dependency system.

---

## Layer Structure

### Layer 1: Financial Systems
- banks
- payment systems
- settlement networks

### Layer 2: Digital Infrastructure
- cloud systems
- telecom networks
- data centres

### Layer 3: Energy Systems
- power grids
- fuel distribution
- utilities

### Layer 4: Logistics Systems
- ports
- transport hubs
- supply chains

---

## Dependency Flow

Financial → Digital → Energy → Logistics

And reverse feedback exists via:
Logistics → Energy → Digital → Financial

---

## Cross-Layer Coupling

Each layer contains:

- internal dependencies (horizontal links)
- external dependencies (vertical links)

This creates a coupled system graph:

G = (V, E)
