Sextant Protocol Technology Platform Power & Systemic Dependency Layer (v1.0)

## 1. Purpose

This appendix extends the Sextant Protocol into large-scale technology platform ecosystems.

It models how concentrated digital infrastructure (cloud, AI, data platforms, payment rails) introduces systemic dependency and asymmetric influence over global economic and operational systems.

---

## 2. Platform Dependency Graph

Define platform network:

P = (U, R)

Where:
- U = set of technology platforms (cloud, AI, data, payment, communication systems)
- R = dependency relationships between economic and institutional systems

---

## 3. Platform Control Influence Function

Define influence of platform p on system node i:

IC(p, i) = α × U_p × D_i × C_i

Where:
- U_p = platform utility dominance
- D_i = dependency level of system i
- C_i = coupling strength between platform and system

---

## 4. Systemic Concentration Risk

Define concentration index:

SCI = Σ (U_p²)

Where:
- higher SCI = fewer dominant platforms controlling majority system dependency

---

## 5. Platform-Induced Cascade Risk

Cascade condition:

If U_p fails → multiple dependent systems fail simultaneously

Formal condition:

Σ D_i × U_p ≥ θ_c

---

## 6. Cross-Domain Amplification

Platform failure impact spreads across:

- financial systems
- logistics systems
- communication systems
- government digital infrastructure

Propagation function:

A(t) = f(P_fail × dependency graph)

---

## 7. Latent Control Asymmetry

Define asymmetry ratio:

A_s = Control(power) / Dependency(resilience)

If:

A_s >> 1 → systemic fragility increases

---

## 8. Interpretation

This layer captures:

- structural dependency on global tech platforms
- systemic concentration of operational control
- cascading failure risk from digital infrastructure
- hidden asymmetry between control and resilience
